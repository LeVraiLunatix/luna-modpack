## 🌙 LUNA 1.3.0

### 🆕 Now on Minecraft 26.1.2 too
- Every edition is built for **26.2** and **26.1.2**, with the same mods wherever they exist.

### 🪶 LUNA Lite
- A lighter edition for modest PCs and laptops: every performance mod, Voxy, maps, Jade, REI and the LUNA theme, without resource packs, shaders or ambience mods.

### 🖥️ LUNA Server
- A ready-to-run Fabric server pack (Lithium, C2ME, VMP, voice chat, graves, day counter…). Vanilla clients can still join.

### ✨ Shaders and Voxy, together
- Sodium and Iris are pinned to the versions where shaders keep rendering Voxy's distant terrain (Sodium 0.9.1 + Iris 1.11.2). With 0.9.2 + 1.11.4, distant terrain turned into sky as soon as a shader was on.
- New shader: **Solas**. **BSL** and **Solas** both draw the Voxy horizon.

### ➕ New mods
- **REI** (recipes and uses), **Freecam**, **Shoulder Surfing Reloaded**, **Simple Voice Chat**, **Flashback** (cinematic recordings, Voxy-aware).
- **Essential** (friends, invite them into your singleplayer world, screenshots) and **Ji AFK Cinematic** (a cinematic camera kicks in when you go AFK).

### 🌙 New look
- New LUNA logo in a blocky, carved-stone style: moonstone lavender, an indigo band, cracks and a crescent carved into the A.
- The title screen is back to the hand-made night panorama: moon, Milky Way, aurora, mirror lake and blocky mountains.

### 🛠️ Fixes and defaults
- No more key conflicts out of the box: the creative "save toolbar" key no longer shares **C** with the zoom, Freecam moves to **F7**, voice chat mute to **F8** and the shoulder swap to **J** (the map stays on **M**, waypoints on **U**).
- Fixed LUNA's first-launch defaults being ignored ("Failed to load options"), which also lost the resource pack order.

### ℹ️ Notes
- Simple Voice Chat, Voxy, C2ME, Nvidium, Sound Physics Remastered and Wakes are beta builds, VMP is alpha: they're the latest versions available.
- Voice chat only works on servers that also have Simple Voice Chat (the LUNA Server pack does).
