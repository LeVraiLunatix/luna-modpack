# 🌙 LUNA

**See further. Play smoother.**

LUNA is a Fabric modpack for **Minecraft 26.2** built around three ideas: squeezing every last frame out of the game, pushing the horizon as far as it can go with **Voxy**, and making the world feel alive with ambience mods, curated resource packs and a hand-made **LUNA theme** on every menu.

It stays close to vanilla: no new blocks, no new items, no new mobs. Same Minecraft, only faster, prettier and bigger.

---

## ⚡ Performance first

LUNA stacks the best optimization mods available for 26.2 and checks that every one of them works with the others:

- **Rendering**: Sodium, Nvidium (NVIDIA cards, switches itself off elsewhere), ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling, MoreCulling, Better Block Entities, BadOptimizations, Particle Core
- **Game logic & worlds**: Lithium, C2ME, ScalableLux, VMP, Structure Layout Optimizer, Alternate Current, Clumps, Ksyxis
- **Memory & startup**: FerriteCore, ModernFix
- **Smoothness**: Dynamic FPS, Ixeris, Gnetum, Krypton, FastQuit

## 🏔️ Look how far you can see

**[Voxy](https://modrinth.com/mod/voxy)** renders the world as lightweight levels of detail (LODs), so mountains and coastlines stay visible far beyond your normal render distance.

> 💡 **Tip:** Voxy only shows terrain that already exists. For the full horizon in a new singleplayer world:
> 1. `/chunky radius 1500` then `/chunky start`
> 2. `/voxy import world "<your world name>"`
>
> Keep your normal render distance low (8–12 chunks) and let Voxy handle the rest.

## 🍃 A world that feels alive

- **Sound**: Sound Physics Remastered (echoes, reverb, muffled sounds), AmbientSounds, Presence Footsteps
- **Visuals**: Falling Leaves, Particle Rain, Wakes, Better Clouds, Subtle Effects, LambDynamicLights, 3D Skin Layers
- **Resource packs**, enabled on first launch: Fresh Animations (+ extensions), Fresh Food, Better Leaves, Better Lanterns, Default Dark Mode, Enchantment Outlines, NewGlowingOres, RAY's 3D Ladders & Rails, Visual Armor Trims, Cubic Sun & Moon, Fullbright UB and more. The engines they need (EMF, ETF, Continuity, Respackopts) are included.
- **Shaders**, off by default: Complementary Reimagined, Complementary Unbound, BSL, Bliss and MakeUp – Ultra Fast

## 🧭 Quality of life

- **Maps**: Xaero's Minimap & World Map, with XaeroPlus extras (press **M**)
- **Info**: Jade (what you're looking at), AppleSkin, a day counter above the hotbar
- **Inventory**: ClientSort (middle-click to sort) and Mouse Tweaks
- **Graves**: your items wait for you in a grave when you die
- **Zoom** with **C** (Zoomify), borderless fullscreen, and Mod Menu to tweak everything

## 🎨 The LUNA theme

Every menu wears the LUNA look: moon-rock logo, night-glass buttons, sliders and tabs, a real 3D panorama captured in-game at sunset over a cherry grove, and new splash texts. It's all textures, so it costs zero FPS.

## 🎵 Sona for Minecraft

LUNA ships with **Sona for Minecraft**, a small companion mod for the Sona music app on Windows: the current track shows up as a vinyl card in the corner, with playback controls and a `/sona` command. If Sona isn't running, the mod simply stays hidden.

## 📥 Installing

1. Install with the **Modrinth App** (or Prism Launcher).
2. Give the instance **at least 6 GB of RAM**.
3. Launch. Your first start applies LUNA's defaults (resource packs in the right order, render distance 12), and **your own settings are never overwritten afterwards**.

**Requirements:** Minecraft Java 26.2 and an OpenGL 4.6 graphics card for Voxy (any recent NVIDIA or AMD card, Intel Arc).
