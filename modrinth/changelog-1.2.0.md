## 🌙 LUNA 1.2.0 — First public release

### ⚡ Performance
- Full optimization stack: Sodium, Lithium, C2ME, ScalableLux, FerriteCore, ModernFix, Nvidium, ImmediatelyFast, Entity Culling, MoreCulling, Better Block Entities, VMP, Ixeris, Gnetum and more
- Voxy for far-distance rendering, plus Chunky to pregenerate the horizon

### 🍃 Ambience
- Sound Physics Remastered, AmbientSounds, Presence Footsteps
- Falling Leaves, Particle Rain, Wakes, Better Clouds, Subtle Effects, LambDynamicLights, 3D Skin Layers

### 🧭 Quality of life
- Xaero's Minimap & World Map with XaeroPlus
- Jade, AppleSkin, ClientSort, Mouse Tweaks
- Day counter above the hotbar, Graves to keep your items on death

### 🎨 Resource packs & shaders
- 20 curated resource packs, enabled automatically on first launch
- Fresh Animations now works properly (Entity Model Features + Entity Texture Features included)
- 5 shader packs, off by default: Complementary Reimagined & Unbound, BSL, Bliss, MakeUp – Ultra Fast

### 🌌 LUNA theme
- New LUNA logo with the "Beyond the horizon" subtitle
- Real 3D title screen panorama, captured in-game at sunset over a cherry grove
- Themed buttons, sliders, tabs, checkboxes and menu backgrounds
- New splash texts

### 🎵 Sona
- Sona for Minecraft included: now playing, playback controls and `/sona` (needs the Sona app on Windows)

### ℹ️ Notes
- Voxy, C2ME, Nvidium, Sound Physics Remastered and Wakes are beta builds and VMP is an alpha: they're the latest versions available for 26.2.
- Graves turns on `keep_inventory` so it can store your items in the grave.
- Default settings only apply to a fresh instance. If you update an existing one, enable the resource packs yourself in Options › Resource Packs and keep **LUNA UI** at the top.
