## 🌙 LUNA 1.3.2

### ⚡ More performance
- **RenderScale**: render the world at a lower resolution while the UI stays sharp — the biggest FPS boost when shaders are on (alpha build).
- **Entity View Distance**: choose how far entities are drawn, great for big bases and farms.
- **Immersive Optimization**: smarter ticking for far-away entities, for a steadier TPS.
- **Voxy World Gen**: generates chunks in the background for Voxy, so the horizon fills in without running Chunky.
- **Quick Pack**: faster loading of zipped resource packs.
- **No Pack Compat Check**: no more "incompatible pack" warning for the 1.21-era packs (26.2 only).

### 🖥️ LUNA Server
- **ServerCore** added: dedicated-server optimizations for chunks, entities and ticking.
